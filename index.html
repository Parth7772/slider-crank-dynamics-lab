    Problem Statement & Notation

    Setting. A slider-crank chain . The crank of radius turns counter-clockwise at  rpm about the fixed centre . The connecting rod has length , mass , centre of mass at from the crank pin and radius of gyration . The piston (bore , reciprocating mass ) slides along the line of stroke. A rim-type flywheel of mean diameter and a counterweight balancing the revolving mass plus a fraction of the reciprocating mass are fitted.

    Coordinates. is the crank angle from inner dead centre (TDC) and the four-stroke cycle angle (0 TDC suction, 180 BDC, 360 firing TDC, 540 BDC). Forces are positive toward the crank; torque is positive in the direction of rotation.

    Data (live).

    crank radius, rod length, obliquity ratio
    crank angle, cycle angle, rod obliquity
    piston displacement, velocity, acceleration
    angular velocity / acceleration of the rod
    gas force, inertia force, piston effort
    thrust in rod, side thrust on cylinder wall
    crank effort (tangential) and radial force
    torque of the correction couple and of rod weight
    max. fluctuation of energy; coefficients of fluctuation of energy and speed
    flywheel inertia, rim mass, width, thickness
    reciprocating and revolving masses, balance fraction

    Assumptions.

    • Rigid links, frictionless joints; crank balanced about .
    • Constant crank speed during the force analysis (the flywheel keeps the ripple within ).
    • Gravity included; single-zone ideal gas with constant ; instantaneous gas exchange at dead centres.
    • Flywheel inertia from the rim alone (conservative).

    Numerics. 1440 crank positions per cycle (0.5°), RK4 for the pressure equation, trapezoidal integrals.
