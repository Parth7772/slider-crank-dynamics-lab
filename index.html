Setting. The engine of Fig. 1 is a slider-crank chain . The crank of radius turns counter-clockwise at  rpm about the fixed centre . The connecting rod has length , mass , centre of mass at distance from the crank pin and radius of gyration . It drives a piston of bore whose reciprocating mass is . The cylinder pressure follows a single-zone first-law combustion model with Wiebe heat release (or an ideal Otto cycle). The crankshaft carries a rim-type flywheel of mean diameter and a counterweight that balances the revolving mass plus a fraction of the reciprocating mass.

To determine.

  1. Kinematics: piston displacement, velocity and acceleration; angular velocity and acceleration of the connecting rod (exact and approximate).
  2. Gas pressure: , indicator diagram, indicated work, mean effective pressure, power and efficiency.
  3. Dynamic force analysis: gas and inertia forces, piston effort, thrust in the connecting rod, side thrust, crank effort, radial force, bearing loads (exact Newton–Euler, rod mass included).
  4. Turning-moment diagram: mean torque, maximum fluctuation of energy , flywheel inertia and rim size for the required .
  5. Balancing: primary and secondary unbalanced forces, shaking force with partial balancing, and the optimum balance fraction.
  6. Sensitivity of the design to every parameter, and design-space maps.
  7. Verification of the solver against independent methods and a textbook problem.

Assumptions.

  • Rigid links, frictionless pin joints and slider; crank balanced about .
  • Constant crank speed during the force analysis (standard practice; the flywheel keeps the ripple within ).
  • Gravity is included; for a horizontal engine the piston weight adds to the side thrust.
  • Single-zone ideal gas with constant ; instantaneous gas exchange at the dead centres; crank-case at atmospheric pressure.
  • Flywheel inertia supplied by the rim alone (hub and arms neglected, which is conservative).

Numerics. 1440 crank positions per cycle (0.5°), RK4 for the pressure ODE, trapezoidal cycle integrals. Grid convergence of the cycle work is better than .
